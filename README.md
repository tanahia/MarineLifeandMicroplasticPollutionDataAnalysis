# Marine Species in Medditerrian and Black Sea and Microplastic Pollution

## About the Project

This project investigates the potential threats of microplastic pollution to marine species in the Mediterranean and Black Seas.

By analysing historical microplastic pollution data (2000–2022) alongside marine species encounter records, the research aims to identify which marine species are potentially exposed to higher levels of microplastic pollution.

The project uses data analysis and machine learning techniques to explore patterns in pollution distribution, examine its relationship with marine species observations and identify areas and species that may require further investigation.
